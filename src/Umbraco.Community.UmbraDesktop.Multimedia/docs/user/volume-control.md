# Volume Control

Volume Control is the desktop's mixer. It has a column for the overall **Volume** and one for each
Multimedia program that makes sound: Media Player, CD Player, Sound Recorder, Camera and Snipping
Tool.

- To turn everything down, move the **Volume** column's slider. Each program plays at its own
  column's level under this one.
- To turn one program down, move its column's slider.
- To silence everything, or one program, select **Mute** under its column. Clearing **Mute** brings
  the sound back at the level it had.

A change applies at once to every open window of that program. Media Player's own volume slider and
**Mute** button are its column here, so changing either moves the other.

Your settings are remembered in this browser, as a computer remembers its volume, and apply to every
backoffice tab in it. They do not follow you to another browser or computer.
